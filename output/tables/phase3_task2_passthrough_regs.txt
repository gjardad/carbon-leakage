 Interpretation (coefficient scale)
----------------------------------------------------------------
log(PPI) and Δ log(PPI) are unitless. Exposure is in share units
(fraction of sector total cost). A coefficient of 1.0 on exp_direct
means a 1-percentage-point rise in sector exposure raises the
sector PPI by ~1 log point (~1%). Full pass-through would give a
coefficient near 1; weaker pass-through gives coefficients < 1.
Negative or noisy near-zero coefficients imply no systematic
pass-through of the ETS carbon-cost shock into sector prices.
