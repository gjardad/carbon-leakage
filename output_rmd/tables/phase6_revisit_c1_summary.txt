Sanity checks vs literature (long-run import-origin sigma):
  BLP (2022) LR trade elasticity: -1.75 to -2.25 (their epsilon^h>=7).
    epsilon = 1 - sigma in our notation, so their sigma in [2.75, 3.25].
  AIK (2014) median sigma across HS-6: ~3.5 (US importers).
  Pure CES with sigma = 1: log(China/nonChina) shares move 1-for-1
    in opposite direction to log(P_China/P_nonChina). Our beta should
    therefore be NEGATIVE (sigma > 1) for substitutes.
